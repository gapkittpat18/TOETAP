TOETAP V2.2.27 — SHOE IMAGE PRESENTATION

Improves real uploaded/AI-processed shoe images.

1. AUTO CROP
After background removal, transparent empty margin is trimmed in the browser.
A small ~4.5% safe padding remains around the shoe.
Existing previously processed photos are not destructively reprocessed; upload/process
again if you want the new crop applied to an old image.

2. BIGGER HOME SHOE
Custom photos use substantially more of the Home hero width/height.
The original fallback TOETAP sneaker keeps its existing presentation.

3. DARK-SHOE VISIBILITY
Custom photos get a subtle neutral radial light halo behind the shoe on the black hero.
This improves edge/detail visibility for black/dark shoes without changing the shoe's
actual colors or replacing the TOETAP black hero.

No SQL migration.
No NFC, ownership, Strava, webhook, matching or activity logic changes.
